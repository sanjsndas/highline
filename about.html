About Highline House Ideas

Highline House Ideas is an independent editorial publication dedicated to the built environment — specifically, the places we live. We publish in-depth articles, practical guides, and critical essays on interior design, residential architecture, materials, lighting, space planning, and sustainable building.

Our mission is simple: to provide useful, well-researched knowledge for homeowners, design enthusiasts, and professionals who care about how spaces are made. We believe good design is not a luxury but a fundamental part of a well-lived life. Whether you are renovating a small apartment, planning a new build, or simply curious about the principles behind a beautiful room, we aim to give you clarity, context, and inspiration.

Editorial Independence

We do not accept sponsored content. Every article is researched, written, and edited by our team. We occasionally use affiliate links, but these never influence our recommendations or editorial judgment. Our only loyalty is to our readers.

We are reader-supported. When you buy through links on our site, we may earn an affiliate commission at no extra cost to you. This helps keep our journalism free and accessible.

Our Team

Highline House Ideas is written and edited by a small, dedicated group of design professionals, journalists, and researchers based in Europe and beyond.

Elena Marchetti — Editor-in-Chief

Architect, author, and former principal at Studio Marchetti in Milan. Elena has over 15 years of experience in residential architecture and interior design. She founded Highline House Ideas to bridge the gap between professional knowledge and everyday homeowners.

James Okonkwo — Senior Editor, Architecture

London-based critic and architectural historian. James writes about residential architecture, urbanism, and the intersection of design and culture. He holds a PhD from the Bartlett School of Architecture.

Clara Lindqvist — Contributing Editor, Interiors

Stockholm-based stylist and writer. Clara covers interior trends, materials, lighting, and the psychology of home. She is passionate about warm minimalism and sustainable living.

What We Cover

Our articles fall into several broad categories, each explored with depth and practical insight:

  • Interior Design — Color, layout, styling, and the principles that make rooms feel cohesive.
  • Architecture — Residential design, passive house, vernacular approaches, and the future of housing.
  • Materials — Stone, wood, tile, countertops, and finishes: how to choose and how they perform.
  • Lighting — Layering, color temperature, fixtures, and the art of illuminating a home.
  • Space Planning — Making small apartments feel generous and large rooms feel intimate.
  • Furniture — Slow furniture, craftsmanship, and investing in pieces that last.
  • Sustainable Design — Energy efficiency, low-impact materials, and climate-responsive building.
