Contact

We welcome thoughtful correspondence from readers, designers, architects, and researchers. Whether you have a question, a pitch, or a correction, we read every message.

Editorial

For story ideas, corrections, or general editorial inquiries:

editors@highlinehouseideas.com

We aim to respond within 2–3 business days.

Pitches

We accept pitches for feature articles and long-form essays on interior design, architecture, materials, lighting, and space planning. Please include a 200-word summary of your proposed piece and a brief note on your background.

Send pitches to: pitches@highlinehouseideas.com

We pay for accepted work. Please allow up to 5 business days for a reply.

Advertising & Partnerships

We do not accept display advertising. However, we are open to select partnership inquiries that align with our editorial values.

For partnership proposals: partners@highlinehouseideas.com
