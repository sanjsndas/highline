Frequently Asked Questions
What is the difference between interior design and interior decoration? +
Interior design involves the planning and construction of interior spaces, including technical elements like lighting, materials, and structural changes. Interior decoration focuses on finishes, furniture, and aesthetics without altering the architecture.
How much does a residential architecture project typically cost? +
Costs vary widely by region and scope. As a general guideline, architectural fees range from 8–15% of construction cost for full-service design. For renovations, expect $150–$400 per square foot depending on finishes and complexity.
What are the most important principles of space planning? +
The core principles are circulation (clear paths), zoning (grouping activities), proportion (scale of furniture to room), and natural light maximization. Good space planning makes a small room feel generous and a large room feel intimate.
How do I choose the right lighting for a room? +
Layer your lighting: ambient (general), task (specific work areas), and accent (feature highlights). Use warm bulbs (2700K–3000K) for living spaces and cooler (3500K–4000K) for kitchens and bathrooms. Dimmers are essential.
What is sustainable architecture? +
Sustainable architecture minimizes environmental impact through energy efficiency, responsible material selection, water conservation, and site-sensitive design. It includes passive design strategies, renewable energy, and durable, low-toxicity materials.
